About me

The person behind Gerald

I'm Shyla Agrawal, 16, and going into my senior year at North Creek High School. I've lived in Bothell for the last ten years, and vehicles going by have been really disturbing for my family for the last two years.

I've always had trouble sleeping, and the road noise doesn't help. It wasn't something we expected to become a problem when we bought our house, but over the last two years it's spiked considerably. That's when I had the idea to analyze road noise and its causes in Snohomish County. Swarali Karale, my research partner, and I spent three months analyzing six years of SnoCo traffic data together, under the supervision of Mr. Jason Rukman, our intermediate data programming teacher, who guided us with feedback throughout.

Where this is headed

What's next

I went to the county just to report what their own data already shows, but I didn't get much of a response. I want to go back with more numbers, so I'm planning to record noise myself from my own window. In the meantime, I'm getting support from the community, one doorstep at a time.
